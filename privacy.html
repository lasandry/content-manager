Privacy Policy: Content Manager

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Content Manager is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel "Code Null". It uses YouTube API Services only to upload, schedule and manage videos on the owner's own channel and to read and reply to comments on those videos. It is not offered to other users.

What data is accessed

  • The owner's YouTube channel ID and title (to confirm the signed-in channel).
  • The owner's own videos, thumbnails and their metadata (title, description, privacy and publish time).
  • Comments posted on the owner's videos (to read and reply to them).

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Comment data is read on demand and not kept in any database.

Deletion

The stored token can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file. Access can also be revoked at Google security settings.

YouTube and Google

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
